# Desktop Detective

**Desktop Detective** is an idle detective RPG that runs in a small window in the corner of your screen while you work, browse, or play other games. Investigate crime scenes, collect loot, craft gear, and rebirth to grow stronger across runs.

Developed by **Conradical Games** and published by **Playsaurus**.

[Desktop Detective on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4225890/Desktop_Detective/)
